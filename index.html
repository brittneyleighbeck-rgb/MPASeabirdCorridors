Why did the birds change?
Tracking reveals different species sampling different parts of the seascape. Food, tuna/dolphin activity, fishing, bycatch, reef condition, and protection interact.
Educational model: Population values are relative indices, not abundance estimates or forecasts. The species' illustrative foraging-radius values are scenario inputs—not PNG measurements. The simulation is intended to demonstrate how seabird tracking can reveal spatial linkages between terrestrial breeding colonies and dynamic pelagic ecosystems, and how those data could inform marine spatial planning and MPA design.
